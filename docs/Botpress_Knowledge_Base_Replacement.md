## Transfer Credit and Prior Learning

Previous study can be an important part of your next degree plan. Velmont’s proposed evaluation process would review eligible university coursework and documented prior learning against the requirements of your chosen program. An official evaluation would explain which credits apply and what remains to complete.

### What previous learning can mean

Eligible evidence and assessment methods will be defined in university policy. Credit would be awarded for demonstrated learning that meets academic requirements; time spent in employment alone does not establish academic credit.

Previous coursework: Prior university study and relevant course records may be considered for alignment with a future program.

Certifications and examinations: Relevant certifications and recognized examinations may provide evidence for evaluation, where policy allows.

Military learning: Recognized military learning may be considered where applicable and supported by the future policy.

Professional experience: Assessed learning demonstrated through professional experience may be considered. Years worked alone do not establish academic credit.

### The proposed evaluation journey

Choose your degree: Evaluation is specific to program requirements.

Review evidence requirements: Approved guidance will identify records, assessment methods, fees and the secure submission route.

Receive an academic evaluation: Reviewers would explain accepted credits, their application to major, general education or elective requirements, and outstanding study.

Review your semester plan: An advisor would help connect the evaluation with prerequisites, course availability, financial planning and the bachelor’s internship requirement.

Request a review where appropriate: Decision notices would explain the approved reconsideration or appeal route.

### Standard and individual completion planning

Your plan will reflect semester terms and the credits that satisfy your chosen degree’s requirements.
