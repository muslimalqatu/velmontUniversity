# Staff readiness checklist

## Academic decisions

- Approve mission, outcomes, curricula, prerequisites, concentration status, semester loads, completion pathways and admissions criteria.
- Establish transfer/prior-learning credit limits, residency, grades, recency, duplicate-learning rules, approved assessments, fees, timing and appeals; decide whether/how prior learning can meet internship requirements.
- Design and approve any accelerated routes or stackable awards before offering them. These remain backlog proposals.

## Operations and partner decisions

- Confirm internship locations, placement responsibility, partner agreements, required hours, prerequisites, supervision, assessment, accessibility and any checks/travel/site expenses. Do not imply paid or nearby placements.
- Define support staffing, contact routes, hours, languages, response expectations and service scope before activation. Distinguish teaching and support languages.
- Establish orientation, engagement activities, progress-review tools/outreach, leave and re-entry procedures.
- Provide verified contact details and approved policies. Do not activate applications, advisor handoffs, portal/email tools or payments before operational readiness.

## Finance decisions

- Approve tuition billing method, required fees and other study/internship expenses; accepted-credit treatment; refunds/withdrawals; installment charges, due dates and eligibility.
- Approve funded awards, grant arrangements, employer partnerships and aid eligibility before publication.
- Staff funding presentation: identify confirmed awards, conditional estimates, competitive opportunities and unavailable funding explicitly. Check amount, period, eligibility, renewal and compatible funding; loans remain repayable. A net-cost estimate must distinguish actual charges and confirmed awards from assumptions.

## Institutional leadership and reporting

- Verify actual IBHE/HLC stage; publish only verified updates. Current copy states intent, not authorization, candidacy or accreditation.
- Confirm proposed mission and the logo artwork wording “Turning Knowledge Into Practice” versus site tagline “Turn knowledge into practice.” No artwork text was changed.
- Establish metric definitions, denominators, ownership, reporting cadence, methods, response rates and privacy thresholds for persistence, progression, completion, re-engagement and post-completion outcomes. Distinguish student groups where meaningful/privacy permits. Publish no invented figures or targets as results.

## Release ownership

- Confirm the GitHub/Netlify production branch and current root publish directory. This source ZIP contained no Netlify configuration or repository metadata; the external hosting settings were not inspectable from the ZIP.
- Replace/re-index Botpress KB and uploaded PDF copies, publish the bot, then test response boundaries with `Botpress_and_PDF_Handoff.md`.
- Recheck the final deployed site and run physical mobile/cross-browser acceptance before external promotion.
